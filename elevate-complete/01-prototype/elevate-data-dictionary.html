11. Calculated KPIs (Derived, Not Input)

Everything computed from the data above. No manual entry needed.

Core dental analytics

OCPSPD = OperatingCostsPerYear ÷ (WeeksOpen × DaysPerWeek × NumberOfSurgeries)
TotalProduction (associate) = AvgDailyProduction × AssocDaysPerYear
AssociateProfit = TotalProduction − Fees − Labs − Materials − (OCPSPD × Days)
ChairOccupancy% = CurrentChairTimeOccupied ÷ TotalChairHoursAvailable × 100
LostRevenuePotential = EmptyChairHours × BenchmarkRevenuePerChairHour

Treatment P&L

PerJourneyProfit = TreatmentValue − AssociateCost − TherapistCost − OperatingCost − FinanceCost − Materials − LabAdjusted
ProfitPerHour = PerJourneyProfit ÷ (TotalMinutes ÷ 60)

Cash flow

FCF = CashFromOps − CapEx − OneOffTax − DebtRepayments
ExcessCash = ClosingCashBalance − (3 × AvgMonthlyOpex) − PlannedTaxes
RunOutDate = FirstMonth(ClosingProjectedBalance < MinimumCashBuffer)

Variance & benchmarking

VariancePct = (ActualExpensePct − BenchmarkPct)
Status: ≤-3% Fantastic · -1↔-3% Slight · -1↔1% Stable · 1↔3% Warning · >3% Take Action

19. Implementation Priority

If you only set up SOME integrations, do these first.

P0Configure Chairs + Org settingsRequired for OCPSPD, chair utilisation, multi-site comparison · 15 min setup
P0Add Associates with split %Even without Dentally, you can run per-associate P&L from manual monthly data
P1Connect Dentally PMSUnlocks per-associate production, treatment volumes, chair utilisation, appointment data, no-shows
P1Customise 97 Treatment Master codes with your pricesPer-treatment profitability requires accurate prices and times
P1Upload first NHS UDA schedule (CSV)NHS practices only · monthly cadence
P2Connect StripeReal-time patient payments + subscription billing
P2Set Insurance + Loan tracking (Wealth)Wealth Builder features · 30 min to enter
P2Compliance baseline (CQC self-assessment)One-off 1-2 hour exercise · then quarterly
P3HR Pro fill-in (sickness, leave, reviews)People management · ongoing
P3Marketing data (ad spend, GBP)If using Marketing Pro features
P4Open Banking (Plaid / TrueLayer)Only if Xero bank feed is unreliable
P4NHS BSA digital integrationWhen BSA releases public API (TBD)
⚡ Minimum Viable Setup: Xero + Chair config + Associates = ~80% of platform value. Total setup time: 1-2 hours.
🚀 Full Setup: All P0–P2 = ~95% of platform value. Total: ~6 hours one-off + ongoing data hygiene.
