Group Overview

Data source Built-in demo data Setup & tabs ▾
Connect your Google Sheet (one-time, ~2 min)
1. Open GM-Group-Data.xlsx in Google Drive (right-click → Open with → Google Sheets).
2. Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer, then copy the sheet link.
3. Paste it above → Connect & load. The pill turns green and every view recalculates from the sheet.
4. Each month: update the sheet's numbers, then hit ⟳ Refresh here.

Tabs the dashboard reads: Practices (id, name, region, chairs, revenue, util, adSpend, wagePct, collectPct, acceptedValue, convStrength, valueIndex, trend, note) · TreatmentMix (practiceId + 9 treatment %, each row = 100) · Entities (academy & lab: revenue, marginPct, adSpend, collectPct, acceptedValue). Keep tab names and id values unchanged. Blank cells keep the built-in value.
